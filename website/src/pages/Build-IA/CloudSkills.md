# Build AI Apps and Agents with the Microsoft Learn AI Skills Fest

---

Gaining AI skills can increase wages by an average of 21%. Build in-demand skills with the Microsoft Learn AI Skills Fest! Master cutting-edge AI technologies like Microsoft Copilot, GitHub, Microsoft Azure, and Microsoft Fabric. Engage in six curated topics, interactive community events, and expert-led sessions to deepen your understanding. Take advantage of a selection of certification offers to showcase your technical knowledge to potential employers. Start your AI journey today!

[Register now](https://aiskillsfest.event.microsoft.com/?ocid=biafy25h1_aiskillschallenge_webpage_azuremktg) to compete and level up your AI skills. AI Skills Fest is live for 50 days of AI discovery and learning **April 8 through May 28!**

## Earn a Microsoft Learn badge 

By completing this challenge before **May 28, 2025**, you will receive a Microsoft Learn **badge** on your Learn profile. These will be delivered to the email address associated with your Microsoft Learn profile. Confirm or add a contact email [here](https://learn.microsoft.com/users/me/settings?ocid=biafy25h1_aiskillschallenge_webpage_azuremktg) to ensure emails are delivered.
